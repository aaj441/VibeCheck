# 📘 Usage Guide: Social Interaction Coach

## Complete Walkthrough for Users

---

## 🌟 Welcome!

This is your personal coach for social interactions. Whether you're preparing for a date, job interview, networking event, or casual gathering, this tool will help you feel more prepared and confident.

---

## 🎯 Who This Is For

- **Neurodivergent individuals** (ADHD, Autism, Social Anxiety)
- **Anyone** who feels anxious about social situations
- **People** who want structured preparation for important interactions
- **Those** who benefit from explicit guidance and practice

## 📝 Complete Session Walkthrough

### Step 1: Context Check-In (2-3 minutes)

**What you'll do:**
- Choose your event type (date, interview, networking, casual, other)
- Describe how you're feeling
- Select the setting (in-person, online, group, one-on-one)
- Say who will be there
- Define what "success" means to you

**Example:**
```
Event Type: Job Interview
Feeling: Anxious
Setting: Online
Participants: "Two interviewers - hiring manager and team lead"
Goal: "Make it to the second round and show my technical skills"
```

**Tips:**
- Be honest about your feelings - this helps me customize support
- Your goal can be as simple as "Get through it without panicking"
- There are no wrong answers

---

### Step 2: Emotional Calibration (3-5 minutes)

**What you'll do:**
- Move 4 sliders to rate yourself (1-10):
  - Anxiety level
  - Excitement level
  - Energy level
  - Focus level
- Add specific worries (e.g., "Forgetting what to say", "Awkward silences")
- List triggers (e.g., "Loud noises", "Being interrupted")
- Optional: Share sensory needs
- Optional: Select neurodivergent type(s)

**Example:**
```
Anxiety: 8/10
Excitement: 4/10
Energy: 6/10
Focus: 5/10

Worries:
- "Freezing when asked unexpected questions"
- "Appearing too nervous"
- "Not knowing when to stop talking"

Triggers:
- "Being put on the spot"
- "Awkward pauses"

Sensory Needs:
- "Need dimmer lighting (bright lights stress me)"

Type: ADHD
```

**What happens next:**
- I generate a personalized checklist based on ALL this information
- High anxiety (7+) triggers extra grounding exercises
- Specific worries get targeted strategies
- Triggers get specific coping phrases

---

### Step 3: Your Personalized Checklist (5-10 minutes)

**What you'll see:**
A customized checklist with 5 categories:

1. **📝 Preparation** - Things to do before the event
2. **🧸 Comfort Items** - What to bring for self-soothing
3. **🧘 Grounding & Affirmations** - Techniques to stay calm
4. **💬 During the Interaction** - Real-time strategies
5. **🚪 Exit Strategies** - How to leave gracefully

**Special indicators:**
- **Purple border** = Customized just for you (based on your triggers/worries)
- **Red left border** = High priority item
- **Checkbox** = Track your progress

**Example items you might see:**

*For high anxiety:*
- "Deep breathing: 4 counts in, hold 4, out 4, hold 4 (repeat 3x)"
- "Bring headphones for before/after (calming music)"

*For "forgetting names" trigger:*
- "Repeat their name when introduced, write it down if possible"

*For "awkward pauses" trigger:*
- "Pauses are normal! Ask an open question or comment on environment"

*For job interviews:*
- "Prepare 3-5 questions to ask interviewer"
- "Practice STAR method answers"
- "Tech check 15 minutes early"

**What to do:**
- Read through everything
- Check off items as you complete them
- Don't stress about completing all items - this is a guide, not a test!

**When ready, click:** "Ready for Practice Scenarios"

---

### Step 4: Practice Scenarios (10-15 minutes)

**What you'll do:**
Practice realistic situations with instant feedback.

**Scenario example for job interview:**
```
Situation: Tell Me About Yourself
Prompt: The interviewer says: "Tell me about yourself."

Sample Responses:
1. "I'm a software developer with 5 years experience..."
2. "I have a background in data science, where I've focused on..."
3. "I'd describe myself as a detail-oriented professional..."

Your turn: Type what you would say
```

**After you respond:**
You get feedback like:
```
✅ Good length - not too brief
✅ Personal touch - you shared about yourself
💡 Consider ending with a question to keep engagement

Remember: There's no single "perfect" response. 
You're practicing and that's what matters!
```

**Navigation:**
- ← Previous: Go back to a scenario
- Skip → : Move to next without answering
- After all scenarios: "I'm Ready for the Real Interaction!"

**Tips:**
- Don't overthink it - respond naturally
- Sample responses are inspiration, not scripts
- Your authentic voice is your best asset

---

### Step 5: Live Interaction Support (Real-time, ongoing)

**When to use:**
- Right before the event
- During the event (if possible)
- If you feel overwhelmed

**How it works:**
Type what's happening and get instant support:

**Example 1: Feeling stressed**
```
You type: "I'm about to go in and I'm really nervous"

You get:
😰 Stressed

Suggestions:
💨 Take a slow, deep breath. You've got this.
⏸️ Say: "Give me just a second to gather my thoughts."
💧 Take a sip of water - natural pause
🤲 Use your fidget/comfort item

Quick Reset:
1. Breathe: 4 counts in, 4 counts out
2. Feel your feet on the ground
3. You're doing well. Stress is normal.
```

**Example 2: Emergency overwhelm**
```
You type: "I'm overwhelmed and can't handle this"

You get:
😵 Overwhelmed

Suggestions:
🛑 Take a break right now. It's okay to excuse yourself.
💬 Say: "Excuse me, I need a moment. I'll be right back."
🚶 Step away to a quiet space

GROUNDING EXERCISE:
1. Place your feet flat on the floor
2. Take 3 deep breaths: in for 4, hold 4, out 4
3. Name 3 things you can see right now
4. Remember: You are safe. This will pass. You're doing your best.
```

**Example 3: Going well**
```
You type: "Things are going pretty well actually!"

You get:
😊 Positive

Suggestions:
🎉 You're doing great! Keep being yourself.
✨ Your authentic energy is showing through
💪 You're handling this well. Be proud!
```

**All your support requests are saved** so you can review them later during reflection.

**When done with the event:** Click "I'm Done - Let's Reflect"

---

### Step 6: Post-Interaction Reflection (5-10 minutes)

**What you'll do:**
Reflect on the experience in a structured way.

**Questions you'll answer:**

1. **What worked well?**
   - Type and add items (at least one)
   - Examples: "I stayed calm", "Asked good questions", "Used breathing technique"

2. **What felt tough?**
   - Optional, but helpful
   - Examples: "Small talk was hard", "Got overwhelmed once", "Struggled with eye contact"

3. **Overall feeling:**
   - Free text
   - Be honest: proud, relieved, disappointed, mixed, etc.

4. **Current energy level** (1-10 slider)
   - How drained/energized are you now?

5. **Would you do this again?**
   - Yes / Not sure or No

6. **Any specific thoughts?** (optional)
   - Anything else you want to note

**What you get back:**

Personalized feedback that:
- ✅ Celebrates your specific successes
- 💪 Acknowledges challenges without shame
- 🎯 Provides actionable next steps
- 💜 Reminds you that progress > perfection

**Example feedback:**
```
🌟 Your Progress Report

What You Did Well:
✅ I stayed calm during tough questions
✅ I asked about team culture
✅ I used my breathing technique when nervous

Growth Areas:
💪 Felt awkward during small talk
*Remember: Challenges are opportunities. You showed up!*

Next Steps:
🛌 Rest & Recharge: Social interactions are taxing. Decompress.
🎧 Engage in a comforting activity
💧 Hydrate and nourish yourself
🏆 Celebrate: You faced a challenge and made it through!

Final Thought:
Progress, not perfection. You're building skills with every 
interaction. Be proud of yourself! 💜
```

**Session complete!** You can start a new one anytime.

## 💡 Tips for Best Results

### Before the Session
- Find a quiet space where you can focus
- Have your calendar/event details handy
- Be honest - this tool can't help if you're not authentic
- No judgment here - share your real feelings

### During the Session
- Take your time - there's no rush
- You can leave and come back later (progress is saved)
- If overwhelmed by the tool itself, step away and return
- Screenshot checklists to reference later

### During the Real Event
- Keep your phone nearby if using live support
- Review your checklist right before
- Remember: You've prepared. You've practiced. You've got this.
- It's okay to not be perfect

### After the Event
- Do reflection when you have energy (not immediately if drained)
- Be kind to yourself in reflection
- Celebrate even "small" wins - they're not small!

## 🎯 Common Use Cases

### Job Interview Preparation
1. **Context**: Choose "interview", set high anxiety
2. **Emotional**: Add worries about answering questions
3. **Checklist**: Get research prompts, question prep, outfit reminders
4. **Practice**: Rehearse "tell me about yourself" and weakness questions
5. **Live**: Use during/before interview for instant confidence boost
6. **Reflect**: Process what worked, plan for next interview

### First Date
1. **Context**: Choose "date", describe your feelings honestly
2. **Emotional**: Share worries about awkward silences or conversation
3. **Checklist**: Get hygiene reminders, conversation starters, boundary phrases
4. **Practice**: Rehearse small talk and getting to know them
5. **Live**: Quick check-ins if nervous during date
6. **Reflect**: Celebrate putting yourself out there

### Networking Event (Especially Hard!)
1. **Context**: Choose "networking", note group setting
2. **Emotional**: High anxiety? Add "starting conversations" worry
3. **Checklist**: Get ice breakers, elevator pitch prep, break reminders
4. **Practice**: Rehearse introducing yourself
5. **Live**: Support when overwhelmed by crowd
6. **Reflect**: Celebrate any meaningful connections made

## 🆘 When Things Go Wrong

### "I'm having a panic attack during the event"
1. Use live support immediately
2. Type: "Panic attack" or "Overwhelmed"
3. Follow the grounding exercise given
4. Give yourself permission to leave if needed
5. Your safety and wellbeing > the event

### "I messed up / said something weird"
- Most people forget small slips immediately
- One awkward moment ≠ total failure
- Use live support: "I said something weird"
- Get reassurance and recovery strategies

### "I can't finish the session - it's too much"
- That's totally okay!
- Your progress is saved automatically
- Come back when you have energy
- You can skip to just the checklist if that's all you need

### "The feedback feels off / not helpful"
- This tool isn't perfect - it's a starting point
- Trust your own judgment over the tool
- Use what helps, ignore what doesn't
- Your experience is valid
